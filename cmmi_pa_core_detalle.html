▶ DOING — Hacer el trabajo técnico
▼ RDM Requirements Dev. & Management RDMPG 1–3 ML 2 Elicitar, analizar y gestionar requisitos del cliente y del sistema. ✔ Verificado
1.1PG 1Record requirements. — Registrar los requisitos.
2.1PG 2Elicit stakeholder needs, expectations, constraints, and interfaces, and confirm understanding of requirements.
2.2PG 2Transform stakeholder needs, expectations, constraints, and interfaces into prioritized customer requirements.
2.3PG 2Obtain commitment from project participants that they can implement the requirements.
2.4PG 2Develop, record, and keep updated bidirectional traceability among requirements and activities or work products.
2.5PG 2Ensure that plans and activities or work products remain consistent with requirements.
3.1PG 3Develop and keep requirements updated for the solution and its components.
3.2PG 3Develop operational concepts and scenarios.
3.3PG 3Allocate the requirements to be implemented.
3.4PG 3Identify, develop, and keep updated interface or connection requirements.
3.5PG 3Ensure that requirements are necessary and sufficient.
3.6PG 3Balance stakeholder needs and constraints.
3.7PG 3Validate requirements to ensure the resulting solution will perform as intended in the target environment.
